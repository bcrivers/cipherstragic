Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Cipher Strategic is a trade name of Accelerated Solutions LLC, an Indiana company ("Cipher Strategic," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through this website and through the Microsoft 365 applications we use to deliver services to clients (Cipher Reporting and Cipher Deploy), and how we handle it.

This website

This site does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or forms. We do not collect personal information when you browse it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those files. If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to manage our relationship with you.

Client applications

Cipher Reporting and Cipher Deploy are Microsoft Entra applications. They only reach an organization's Microsoft 365 tenant after an administrator of that organization grants consent, and only for clients with an active engagement with us.

  • Cipher Reporting has read-only access. It reads tenant configuration, user and device inventory, license usage, and service health data so we can produce client reports and recommendations.
  • Cipher Deploy makes configuration changes a client has asked us to make, such as device management policies and mail settings.

Neither application reads the contents of email, files, or chats unless a specific engagement requires it and the client has agreed to that in writing.

How we use and protect data

  • We use client data only to deliver the services the client engaged us for.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share client data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Reports and working files are stored in our own Microsoft 365 environment, with access limited to Cipher Strategic consultants working on the engagement.
  • Application credentials are certificate-based and stored on managed devices.

Retention and removal

We keep client data for the length of the engagement and as needed to meet legal or accounting obligations. When an engagement ends, we delete client data on request. A client administrator can remove our access at any time by deleting the Cipher Reporting or Cipher Deploy enterprise application from their tenant.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
