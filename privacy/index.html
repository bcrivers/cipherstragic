Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Cipher Strategic ("we," "us") collects through this website and through the applications we use to deliver services to clients, and how we handle it.

This website

This site does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or forms. We do not collect personal information when you browse it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those files. If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to manage our relationship with you.

Client applications

To deliver our services, we use applications that connect to a client's Microsoft 365 environment. They only have access after an administrator of that organization grants consent, and only for clients with an active engagement with us. We use that access to review configuration and produce reports, and to make changes the client has asked us to make.

How we use and protect data

  • We use client data only to deliver the services the client engaged us for.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share client data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Access to client data is limited to Cipher Strategic consultants working on the engagement.

Retention and removal

We keep client data for the length of the engagement and as needed to meet legal or accounting obligations. When an engagement ends, we delete client data on request. A client administrator can remove our access at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
